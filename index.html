How to play

Drag anywhere to steer your spark. It moves the same way your finger does, so you can keep your thumb off to the side.

Every shape warns you first: faint outlines grow brighter, then fire on the beat. Get out of the way before it lands.

You have hearts. Lose them all and you restart from the last checkpoint. On a keyboard, use arrow keys or WASD. P pauses.
